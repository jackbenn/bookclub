{{ club.display_name }} is a book club that picks what to read next together each month. Anyone in the club can nominate a book; everyone marks the ones they'd be happy reading; the site runs an election to pick the winner.

This site handles the whole cycle: nominating books, marking your approvals, and running the monthly election. It also keeps a record of everything the club has read.

The goal of the election system

A simple "most votes wins" system rewards whichever taste is most common in the group and leaves everyone else picking books they didn't ask for, month after month. The goal here is different: over time, everyone's taste should get a fair, roughly proportional share of picks — not just the majority's. If a few members share a taste that's a minority in the group, they should still see their kind of book win about as often as their share of the club would predict.

Concretely, that means: nominate freely, mark every book you'd genuinely enjoy (there's no benefit to being stingy), and trust that the system remembers whose picks have won recently and gives everyone else a turn.

How the vote actually works

Each member has an influence weight, starting at 1.0 when they join. Every active book's score for the month is the sum of the weights of the members who marked it as a book they'd read. The book with the highest score wins.

This is a form of Phragmén's sequential method, a proportional voting rule. What makes it proportional is what happens after each win:

  • Every member who approved the winning book gets a bit more load — their weight for future months drops, in proportion to how much influence they just spent.
  • Members who were active that month (i.e. visited the site) then get their load reduced by a decay factor, so weight for people who keep participating gradually recovers even after a win.
  • Members who weren't active that month are left alone — their load neither grows nor shrinks, so people who've drifted away don't quietly build up outsized influence for a future month they come back to vote in.

Repeated over many months, this is what produces the fairness guarantee: a consistent minority ends up winning roughly as often as its size in the club would predict, without anyone needing to coordinate or strategize.

Secrecy and timing

Your approvals are private while voting is open — nobody, including other members, can see who marked what. That's deliberate: it stops people from waiting to see what's popular and piling onto the front-runner, or avoiding a book because of who else likes it. Approvals can be changed at any time before the month is finalized.

An admin finalizes each month once voting closes. At that point the winner and the two runners-up are revealed on the results page — individual approvals stay private even after finalization, and weighted scores are never shown to non-admins.

Tiebreakers

Exact ties are rare, but when weighted scores match, the tie is broken in order by: raw (unweighted) approval count, then page count (the shorter book wins), then nomination date (the older nomination wins, as a small reward for patience).
